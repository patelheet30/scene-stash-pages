Common questions

My stash isn't on my new iPhone

Sign in with the same Apple Account and check iCloud is on for Scene Stash in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. In the app, Settings › iCloud shows when it last synced. A big stash can take a few minutes the first time.

Premiere alerts don't arrive

Turn on notifications for Scene Stash in iOS Settings › Notifications, then switch on Premiere alerts in the app's Settings. Alerts come at six on the evening a new episode lands, and three days before a season premiere, for shows in your stash.

Now Showing isn't on my Lock Screen

Check Live Activities are allowed in iOS Settings › Scene Stash. Then open a film and tap Start now.

A runtime or credits scene is wrong

Film information comes from TMDB, which anyone can edit. Fix it on themoviedb.org and Scene Stash picks it up, or send us a ticket.

Where to watch shows the wrong services

Choose your region and services again under Watching from in the app's Settings. Availability comes from JustWatch and changes often.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app, then remove its iCloud data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Scene Stash.
