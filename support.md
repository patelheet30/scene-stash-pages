# Scene Stash support

Scene Stash tracks the films and shows you watch: a watchlist, episode punch cards, ratings, marathons and a programme for tonight.

## Get help

Open an issue at [github.com/patelheet30/scene-stash-pages/issues](https://github.com/patelheet30/scene-stash-pages/issues). Say which iPhone and iOS version you're on.

## Common questions

**My stash isn't on my new phone.**
Sign in to the same Apple ID and make sure iCloud is on for Scene Stash (Settings › [your name] › iCloud). Settings › iCloud in the app shows when it last synced.

**Premiere alerts don't arrive.**
Turn on notifications for Scene Stash in iOS Settings › Notifications, and switch on Premiere alerts in the app's Settings.

**Where does the film and show information come from?**
[TMDB](https://www.themoviedb.org). Streaming availability is provided by JustWatch. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## Privacy

See the [privacy policy](privacy). Scene Stash has no accounts, analytics or ads.
