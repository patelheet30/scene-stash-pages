01 · Tonight

Your evening, already programmed

Open the app and tonight is laid out: the next episode of everything you're watching, the film you're halfway through, and a pick from your watchlist, timed from doors to lights up.

02 · Stay seated

Know before the credits roll

Every film tells you if there's a scene during or after the credits. Stopped halfway? Drag the film strip to where you are and pick up from there later.

03 · Punch cards

Every episode, punched

Every season is a punch card. Punch off episodes as you go and always know what's next, and when the next one lands.

04 · Now showing

It keeps time. Pause for ads.

Tap Start now and a Live Activity keeps time on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Pause for an ad break and the end time moves with you.

05 · Widgets

Tonight, on your Home Screen

Widgets for tonight's programme, the next premiere and your punch card, so you know what's on without opening the app.
