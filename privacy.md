# Scene Stash privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Scene Stash keeps track of the films and shows you watch. It's built to know as little about you as possible.

## What stays with you

- **Your stash** (what you've tracked, watched, rated, your notes, marathons and where you are in a film) is stored on your device. If iCloud is on, it syncs through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We can't see it.
- **Preferences** such as your region and streaming services stay on your device.

## What leaves your device

- **Film and show information** comes from [The Movie Database (TMDB)](https://www.themoviedb.org). To show you titles, posters, cast, seasons and where to watch, the app sends TMDB the title IDs you open, your search terms, and your region. It doesn't send your name, email, or anything that identifies you. TMDB's own [privacy policy](https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy) applies to those requests.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no sign-up.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or data brokers.
- No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

## Notifications

Premiere alerts are scheduled on your device from your own stash. Nothing is sent to a server to do it.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. To remove synced data, delete Scene Stash's data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.
