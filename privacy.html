Scene Stash keeps track of the films and shows you watch. It's built to know as little about you as possible. This policy covers the Scene Stash iPhone app and this website, both run by Heet Patel in the United Kingdom.

What stays with you

  • Your stash: what you've tracked, watched and rated, your notes, marathons and where you are in a film. It's stored on your iPhone and, if iCloud is on, synced through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. We can't see it.
  • Preferences such as your region and streaming services stay on your device.

What leaves your device

Film and show information comes from The Movie Database (TMDB). To show titles, posters, cast, seasons and where to watch, the app sends TMDB the title IDs you open, your search terms and your region. Like any website, TMDB sees your IP address. It doesn't get your name, email or anything else that identifies you. TMDB's privacy policy covers those requests. Streaming availability reaches the app through TMDB, from JustWatch.

What we don't do

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or data brokers.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.

Notifications

Premiere alerts are scheduled on your iPhone from your own stash. Nothing is sent to a server to do it.

This website and cookies

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other sites. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Scene Stash collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone. To remove synced data, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Scene Stash and delete its data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub. Under UK data protection law you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
